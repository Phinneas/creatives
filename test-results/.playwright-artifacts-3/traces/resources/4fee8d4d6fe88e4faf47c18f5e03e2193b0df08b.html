Why The Juice Index

Built for drivers, not networks.

Independently scored

We rank every network on measured reliability — never on who pays us. No sponsored placements, ever.

Field-verified

Station pages include editorial field intelligence — amenities, cell signal, winter access — that the apps miss.

Backed by 14M+ sessions

Scores are computed from millions of anonymized charging sessions — real outcomes, not spec sheets.

Price transparency

Up-front $/kWh including idle and session fees, so the number you see is the number you pay.
